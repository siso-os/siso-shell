Why this template exists

his words · the reading · the ceilings

  1. His words 1

    verbatim, from 00_AGENT_ZERO/source/ — never paraphrased

    all the different God questions, which are different allocations of compute, like we need a template for each and every God question project, right? Because it will show a bunch of stuff.Shaan · 6 Sep 16:36

  2. Reading authored by the seat

    His 16:36: 'all the different God questions, which are different allocations of compute, like we need a template for each and every God question project, right? Because it will show a bunch of stuff.' A GQ page is where a decision target meets its evidence and its compute.

    Readers. Human: what are we trying to learn, what do we know, what is it costing. Agent: the decision target + counterexample rule + which modules to run next.

    1. What is the exact question and the decision it changes?
    2. What is the current answer and its counterexample?
    3. What evidence exists, dated, with receipts?
    4. Which compute modules are allocated / done / unallocated?
    5. What would close it?

  3. What this page may claim

    Claim ceilingEvidence lines cite a receipt path; 'answer' is labelled proposed until a receipt supports it; ratios carry n.
    Data it rendersGOD-QUESTIONS.md card, plan/modules.json rows with domain god-questions, domains/compute/runs// receipts (U19), source/ words that raised it.
    PrivacyPublic.
    Empty stateNo run yet → 'no receipt; allocated to ' or 'unallocated'.
