  2. Reading authored by the seat

    Good software already contains years of hard-won domain knowledge. Foundry makes that work discoverable and tests how it could serve a real business need, so each project does not have to start its research again. The Library preserves the resulting identities and receipts; Knowledge owns durable retrieval, and the product owner still decides what to adopt. (reading.why, authored by LIBRARY-ZERO)

    Readers. Human: what is it, is it worth opening, what is the sauce. Agent: entry file, commands, owner, what to verify.
